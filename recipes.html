Samp & Tripe

Preparation: 20 minutes | Cooking: 1 hour

Ingredients

  • Samp
  • Tripe
  • Onion
  • Tomatoes
  • Stock
  • Salt and pepper
  • Seasoning

Instructions

  1. Clean and prepare the tripe according to the recipe requirements.
  2. Cook the samp until tender.
  3. Cook the tripe with onion, tomatoes and seasoning.
  4. Allow the ingredients to simmer together.
  5. Serve the samp and tripe warm.

Cheesecake

Preparation: 25 minutes | Chilling: 4 hours

Ingredients

  • Cream cheese
  • Biscuits
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Vanilla
  • Fresh cream

Instructions

  1. Crush the biscuits and mix with melted butter.
  2. Press the mixture into a cake tin.
  3. Prepare the cream cheese filling.
  4. Pour the filling over the biscuit base.
  5. Refrigerate until set.
  6. Serve chilled.
